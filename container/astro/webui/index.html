Wi-Fi

The head keeps its own access point and also joins your network, so it is reachable without switching Wi-Fi. Both radios share one channel — joining forces the AP onto your network's channel.

Sixty seconds after the last client disconnects the head powers its radio down and the network disappears. Holding the control link open prevents that, at the cost of battery — and, while the mount is not aligned, of making the Benro app ask for a compass calibration.
